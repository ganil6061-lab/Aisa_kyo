Science Reel · 01

Rang alag-alag
kyun dikhte hain?

Chalo 40 second mein science samajh lete hain 👇

Step 01

Safed roshni 7 rangon ka mixture hai

  • Laal700 nm
  • Narangi620 nm
  • Peela580 nm
  • Hara530 nm
  • Neela470 nm
  • Jamuni440 nm
  • Baingani410 nm

Har rang ki apni wavelength — 700nm se 400nm tak

Step 02

Object ka rang = jo reflect hota hai

Seb baaki 6 rang nigal leta hai — sirf laal wapas bhejta hai

Step 03

Roshni badlo — rang badal jaayega

Hari roshni mein laal seb ke paas reflect karne ko kuch nahi 🖤

Step 04

Aakash neela, shaam laal — kyun?

Din · Neela bikharta hai
Shaam · Lamba raasta, laal

Chhoti wavelength (neela) zyada scatter hoti hai — isliye aakash neela

Conclusion

Rang cheezon mein nahi,
roshni mein hota hai.

Object jo rang reflect karta hai — wahi tumhari aankh tak pahunchta hai.
